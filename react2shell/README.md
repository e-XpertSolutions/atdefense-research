## At-Defense react2shell

This repo contains various information and tools developped and used by our SOC At-Defense to protect our customers against react2shell vulnerability (CVE-2025-55182). This material is provided for free for anyone to use, without any garantee or support. 

- Identify potential vulnerable Linux systems: [react2find](https://github.com/e-XpertSolutions/atdefense-research/tree/master/react2shell/react2find.sh)
- Identify potential vulnerable Windows systems: [react2find](https://github.com/e-XpertSolutions/atdefense-research/tree/master/react2shell/react2find.ps1)
